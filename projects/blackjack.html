B. Final decision: Console Blackjack

What is the program going to do?

You can play blackjack against a computer dealer, you choose how many chips to bet, then you hit or stand, the dealer flips over one card, then you hit or stand, the program draws chips, and then it plays itself.

Who will it attract?

. Classmates, friends or anyone who loves card games and would like to play without any possibility of losing real money.

What do you want to know?

How is the player called? The number of starting chips the person wants to bet for each round. Stand, continue?

What decisions will my program make?
  • Was the wager valid ( than 0. No greater than their chips)?
  • Did the player go over 21 (bust)?
  • "Hit (17-=)"? Stand (17+=)?
  • Player, dealer, or tie, who wins?

. Types of Data

Variable Type Purpose
name String Players name, used in output
Chips int Bankroll which is updated per each round
bet int Amount bet
playerTotal int Updated on each hit of player hand
dealerTotal int Dealers hand
choice String "h" or "s"
winRate double Wins / Rounds Played
playing boolean Continue the game loop

Class other than the Runner, Player with name, chips and hand total and methods to add card, check for a bust win or loose the bet.

C. Decompose the project

Beginning

  • Welcome user: println "Welcome to Console Blackjack! Beat the dealer without going over 21."
  • Show Directions: press print rules Face cards are worth 10 an ace is 11 or 1 the dealer hits until 17 "h" means hit and "s" means stand.
  • Example of setup: prompt, "Please enter your name:" & "How many chips do you want to start with?" and then create the Player object.

Main program

  • (a) Place a bet: ask "You have X chips. Please enter your bet:". (If the bet is less than 1 or more than their chips, display an error message and Ask again).
  • Dealing: give each player two cards and deal two cards to the dealer. Reveal each player's total and one dealer card.
  • Player turn: ask, "Hit or stand? (h/s)".
    If h, draw a card and insert it into playerTotal. If the total is over 21, println "Bust!". Finish the game.
    If s, move on to the dealer.
  • Dealer turn: card is added to the dealerTotal while the value is less than 17.
  • Determine who wins:
    If the player busted the dealer wins
    Else if dealer busted player wins
    Else if > dealerTotal the player wins
    Else if playerTotal
    Otherwise it's a push (a tie) and the stake will be refunded
  • Update chips: add the bet on a win subtract it on a loss no change on a push.
  • Class Player: instance variables: name, chips, handTotal class methods: addCard(), isBust(), winBet(), loseBet(), getName(), getChips(), getHandTotal(). All methods should do exactly what you would expect.

Output / end

  • Feedback: "Alex you win 25 chips! You are at 125."
  • Play again: ask " another round? (y/. ". Stop if the answer is n or the player has 0 chips.
  • Final result: print the chips compared to the original chips rounds won and win rate then a closing message, like "You finished up 40 chips nice job!" the house wins this time.
